TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

3. Cement
3.1 Type: Ordinary Portland Cement 43 Grade conforming to IS 8112:1989 shall be supplied.
3.2 Strength: The 28 days compressive strength shall not be less than 43 MPa. The initial setting time shall not be less than 30 minutes and final setting time shall not be more than 600 minutes.
3.3 Fineness: Fineness of cement shall not be less than 225.
3.4 Make: Cement shall be of reputed brand such as UltraTech or Ambuja only.
3.5 Packing: Cement shall be packed in 50 kg bags with ISI mark and the BIS licence number printed on each bag.

4. Reinforcement Steel
4.1 Material: TMT bars shall be high quality, durable and conform to IS 1786:2008 Grade Fe 500D, in nominal diameters of 12 mm, 16 mm and 25 mm.
4.2 Mechanical Properties: The 0.2% proof stress shall not be less than 500 N/mm2 and the tensile strength shall not be less than 565 N/mm2.
4.3 Ductility: Percentage elongation shall not be less than 12%.
4.4 Chemical Composition: Carbon content shall not exceed 0.18% and Sulphur shall not exceed 0.040%.
4.5 Bend Test: Bend and rebend tests shall be carried out as per relevant IS codes.

QUALITY ASSURANCE AND TESTING

7. Testing
7.1 Test certificates from the manufacturer should be submitted along with each consignment.
7.2 Ductility Limit: To ensure ductility, the yield stress of bars shall not exceed 480 N/mm2.
7.3 Each lot of cement shall be tested for 28 days compressive strength prior to dispatch, and the test report shall accompany the consignment.

8. Inspection
8.1 The Engineer-in-Charge may inspect the material at the manufacturer's works or at site as required.

9.3 The entire quantity shall be delivered within fifteen days from the date of issue of the Purchase Order.
