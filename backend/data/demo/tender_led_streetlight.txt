DEMO TENDER — FOR SIH PS-108 DEMONSTRATION ONLY (FICTIONAL BUYER)
MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DEMO CITY — ELECTRICAL DEPARTMENT
NOTICE INVITING TENDER No. DEMO/MC/ELE/2026/077
Supply of LED Street Light Luminaires for Ward 12

1. General
1.1 Name of Work: Supply of 120 W LED street light luminaires.
1.2 Date of Publication: 02-11-2026
1.3 Pre-bid Meeting: 10-11-2026
1.4 Last Date of Bid Submission: 30-11-2026

2. Technical Specifications
2.1 Standard: Luminaires shall conform to IS 10322 (Part 5/Sec 1):2012 including all amendments in force on the last date of bid submission, and shall carry BIS registration under the Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS); the R-number shall be furnished with the bid.
2.2 Power: Rated power shall be 120 W with a tolerance of ± 5%.
2.3 Efficacy: System luminous efficacy shall not be less than 130 lm/W, measured as per IS 16106.
2.4 Ingress Protection: The luminaire shall be IP66 as per IS/IEC 60529.
2.5 Power Factor: Power factor shall not be less than 0.95.
2.6 Surge Protection: Surge protection shall not be less than 10 kV.

3. Testing and Acceptance
3.1 Type test reports from a NABL-accredited laboratory, not older than 3 years, shall be submitted with the bid.
3.2 Acceptance: Sampling for acceptance tests shall be as per the relevant Indian Standard; a lot failing any acceptance test on re-test shall be rejected.

4. Delivery and Warranty
4.1 Delivery shall be completed within 60 days from the date of issue of Purchase Order.
4.2 Warranty: The luminaires shall carry a warranty of not less than 60 months from the date of commissioning.
